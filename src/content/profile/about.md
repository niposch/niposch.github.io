I'm Nick, a software developer based in Germany. I have a B.Sc. in Computer
Science (*Informatik*) and am currently finishing my M.Sc. in Applied Computer
Science (*Angewandte Informatik*), alongside five years of professional
experience as a working student (*Werkstudent*).

## What I do at work

My core day-to-day stack is **C# / .NET** on the backend and **TypeScript**
with **Angular** on the frontend, along with **SQL**, **Azure DevOps**
pipelines, and automated testing in **Playwright** and **Vitest**. Outside of
that, I'm also comfortable writing **Vue**, **React**, **Python**, and **Rust**
by hand.

A big part of my work involves living inside long-running and legacy
environments (including VB6), integrating new features carefully without
breaking existing workflows while making enterprise software genuinely nicer to
use. I enjoy owning features end to end, from architecture and testing down to
the UI/UX details customers actually feel.

Good developer experience matters to me too. When a local workflow gets in the
way, I'd rather fix it than work around it. For example, I built internal
launcher tooling at work so multiple Git worktrees of a heavy application can
run cleanly side by side on one laptop without port collisions or spinning up
several heavyweight IDE instances.

## Working with AI coding tools

I'm upfront about using LLMs extensively, both at work and on most of the
recent side projects on this site. At work, pairing with coding agents has
helped me ship features that landed well with customers on both UI/UX and
reliability. Getting AI-assisted software to the point where it actually just
works isn't as simple as firing off a prompt: it still takes clear scoping,
taste, knowing how existing systems fit together, and verifying everything
properly.

On personal projects, coding agents let me move quickly across stacks and
domains well outside my day job (from Windows display internals and Linux
scanner drivers to Android TV forks and Godot prototypes). My focus there is
steering the design, debugging, and testing on real hardware until I actually
want to use the result every day. Older projects here, like the Ludum Dare game
jams and the Unturned kit selector, are 100% hand-written.

## Outside of work

When I'm not studying or working, I run a self-hosted homelab, occasionally
join Ludum Dare game jams with friends, and build whatever small utility or
game catches my interest. This site is where I collect the stuff worth sharing.
